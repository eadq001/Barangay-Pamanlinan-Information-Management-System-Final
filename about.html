Figure No. 1. Official Seal — Barangay Pamanlinan

The official seal of Barangay Pamanlinan embodies symbols that represent the livelihood, natural resources, and identity of its people. At the center stands a tree with abundant growth — symbolizing agriculture and the barangay’s deep connection to nature. A shrimp is also depicted, representing fishing and aquaculture, one of the main sources of income in Purok 5 and 6 where fishponds thrive.

The color green reflects fertility and sustainability, while yellow signifies hope and optimism for a bright future. Together, these elements reflect the simplicity, resilience, and close relationship of the people with their natural surroundings.
